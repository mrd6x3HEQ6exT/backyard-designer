Workflow

1. Draw the yard outline first (Draw areas → Yard). Click grid points; click the first point or press Enter to close. Every edge is dimensioned automatically and updates when you drag a vertex.
2. Add fixed things: patio, house wall, hose bib, fence, planter wall.
3. Place sprinkler heads, set radius/arc/zone in Properties. Check the Zones tab against your bucket-test GPM.
4. Draw pipe, drip, conduit and wire runs. Fittings are counted automatically at bends.
5. BOM tab lists everything with editable prices. Export JSON to save; PNG to print.

Mouse

Wheel = zoom. Middle-drag, right-drag or Space+drag = pan. In Select: drag to move, drag a vertex to reshape, drag the square handle to resize, click an edge midpoint (+) to add a vertex, Alt+click a vertex to delete it.

Data notes

Head radius/GPM figures are manufacturer nominal values at the listed pressure; GPM scales with arc. Verify against the nozzle chart for your actual pressure. Orbit 50020 kit requires ≥50 PSI and ≥5 GPM at the hose bib.
Conduit rules of thumb (NEC): ≤360° of bends between pull points; PVC branch circuits buried 18" to top of conduit (12" if GFCI-protected 20A or less, 120V — check local code).
